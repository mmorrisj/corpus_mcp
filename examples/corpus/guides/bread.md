# Sourdough

## Hydration

Hydration is the weight of water divided by the weight of flour. A seventy
percent hydration dough is slack and open-crumbed; a sixty percent dough is
stiff and much easier for a beginner to shape.

## Feeding a starter

Feed a starter equal weights of flour and water every twelve to twenty-four
hours at room temperature. A starter kept in the refrigerator needs feeding
only once a week.

## Oven spring

Steam in the first ten minutes keeps the crust soft so the loaf can expand. A
cast iron pot traps the loaf's own moisture and produces the same effect
without a steam-injected oven.
