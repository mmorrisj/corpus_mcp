# Pour-over brewing

## Ratio

A 1:16 ratio of coffee to water by weight is the standard starting point.
Twenty grams of coffee to three hundred and twenty grams of water yields a
balanced cup for most light roasts. Move toward 1:15 for a heavier body.

## Water temperature

Brew between ninety-two and ninety-six degrees Celsius. Cooler water
under-extracts and tastes sour; boiling water scalds the grounds and pulls
bitter compounds out of the coffee.

## Grind size

Espresso needs a fine grind because water passes through the puck in under
thirty seconds. Immersion methods such as the French press need a coarse grind
because contact time runs to four minutes.
