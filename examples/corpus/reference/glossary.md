# Glossary

**Extraction** — the process of dissolving soluble compounds out of ground
coffee. Under-extraction tastes sour and thin; over-extraction tastes bitter
and hollow.

**Hydration** — in baking, the ratio of water to flour by weight.

**Bulk fermentation** — the first rise, ending when dough has risen by roughly
fifty percent and feels airy. Judging by time alone fails because dough
temperature changes fermentation speed dramatically.

**Oven spring** — the rapid expansion of a loaf in the first minutes of baking.
